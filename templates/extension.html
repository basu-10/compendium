What it does

Capture the web into Compendium

The extension turns passive browsing into structured evidence. Open a popup, pick where the page belongs, and it lands as an asset right behind the claim it backs up.

◉

One-click save

Click the toolbar icon to open the capture popup. The current page's title and content are already loaded — just choose a destination and save.

☷

Right-click capture

Use the "Save to Compendium" context menu on any page, link, image, or selection. The page is stashed and ready the moment you open the popup.

☼

Pick the destination

File the clip into a domain, folder, topic, and the exact statement it supports. Your last choice is remembered so repeat captures are even faster.

▧

Tag as you go

Add comma-separated tags while capturing so the asset is organised the instant it reaches your knowledge base, not later when you've forgotten the context.

■

Stored as richtext

Captured pages arrive as richtext assets, so the formatting, quotes, and structure you read are preserved next to the statement they prove.

▲

Keeps the trail

Because the clip attaches to a specific statement, the source never drifts away from the claim. Six months later, the reasoning and the proof are still together.

Install

Running the extension in three steps

Compendium Capture installs unpacked — no Chrome Web Store needed. Download the files, point Chrome at the folder, and you're capturing.

  1. 1

    Download the files

    Grab the bundle and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it. You'll load that folder directly into Chrome.

    Download extension
  2. 2

    Load unpacked

    Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode (top right), and click Load unpacked. Select the unzipped compendium-capture folder.

  3. 3

    Point it at your server

    The extension talks to Compendium over localhost. Make sure your Compendium instance is running, then click the icon on any page to start saving.

The extension connects to a Compendium server on your machine. If your instance runs on a different host or port, update host_permissions in manifest.json to match before loading it.
