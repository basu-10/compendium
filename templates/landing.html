The problem

Research falls apart between the tabs

Notes live in one app, PDFs in a folder, the spreadsheet that proves the point in another. By the time you write it up, the reasoning is there but the evidence trail is gone. Compendium keeps the two together from the start.

■

Organise by domain

Start with the broad areas you work in — a field, a market, a body of literature. Each domain is a home for everything you learn about it.

□

Break it into topics

Topics are the questions you're actually trying to answer. They keep a domain from becoming another folder you never open.

●

State the claim

Write what you believe to be true as a plain statement. Claims are first-class, not buried in a paragraph of notes.

◎

Attach the proof

Hang links, documents, images, video, tables, and long-form notes off each claim. Open any statement and see exactly what supports it.

▣

Side-by-side review

Claims on the left, their evidence on the right. Click a statement and the evidence pane follows, so you never lose your place.

▲

Built to revisit

Come back in six months and the argument still holds up, because the reasoning and the sources were never separated.

How it works

Four levels, one thread

  1. 1

    Create a domain

    The widest container — the subject you keep coming back to.

  2. 2

    Add topics

    Narrow the domain into specific questions worth investigating.

  3. 3

    Write statements

    Record each claim, finding, or conclusion in its own right.

  4. 4

    Attach evidence

    Back every statement with the sources that justify it.
